Continuous Auth

Continuous authentication using CNN+LSTM keystroke dynamics with WebAuthn passkey step-up verification.

Keystroke Dynamics (CNN + LSTM)

Per-user model fine-tuned from a base model trained on 148 participants. Monitors dwell time and key-transition intervals in real time.

WebAuthn Passkey Step-Up

When rhythm deviation is detected, a FIDO2 passkey challenge is issued. Genuine users verify in seconds; intruders are blocked.

Adaptive Learning

A clean stretch of typing is offered as a training sample. After the number of samples the organisation sets (3 by default), the user retrains their personal model with their passkey.
